Delicious Vegetable Pasta

A simple and tasty homemade recipe

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup chopped vegetables
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons tomato sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated cheese

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot and cook the pasta until soft.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and add chopped garlic.
  3. Add the vegetables and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt, and pepper.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything well.
  6. Garnish with grated cheese and serve hot.

Enjoy your homemade vegetable pasta!
